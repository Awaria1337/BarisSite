### Review themes (from 5 most-relevant 5-star reviews in the Maps data, 3–5 months old)
- **Barış Bey personally** is named and trusted. He gives honest advice, does no unnecessary treatments, and does what suits the hair ("Gereksiz hiç bir işlem yapılmadı").
- **Coloring & styling** turned out exactly as wanted, "hayal ettiğimin çok daha üstünde".
- **Hair extensions (kaynak)**: a natural result that blends with the client's own hair; strong craftsmanship. This is a highlighted specialty.
- **Modern short cuts** (pixie-type) that did not make the client look older.
- **Team** is warm, smiling ("güler yüzlü ekip") and stays polite with difficult customers.
- Customers coming from abroad (yurt dışı) and from other salons say they found Barış Alaş through the reviews.
- Low-star review themes: not seen in the sample (only the top reviews were accessible).
